Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

It depends on your target, your starting amount and your date. Enter those three figures in the calculator and it will give you the monthly deposit, after allowing for interest at the rate you enter.

What interest rate should I enter?

Enter the rate your own savings account actually pays. Rates vary by bank and account, so do not use a figure from a guide as your rate.

Can I reach a goal faster by saving more?

Yes. Raising the monthly deposit is the fastest way to shorten the time to your goal, and the time-to-goal calculator shows how many months you save.

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on interest. Each month your balance grows, and the next month you earn interest on the larger balance.

Is it better to save a little every month?

Usually yes. A small regular deposit you can keep is better than a large deposit you abandon after a few months.

How do I save for a house deposit?

Set the deposit target, the date you want it by, and your current savings, then use the goal calculator to find the monthly deposit.

What if my income changes?

Re-run the calculation with the new deposit or the new date. A plan that adjusts is a plan you can keep.

Do I need an emergency fund first?

Most advisers suggest a small emergency fund before long-term saving, so an unexpected bill does not force you to raid the goal fund.
