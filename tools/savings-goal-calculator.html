Savings Goal Calculator

Plan how much to save each month to reach a target by your chosen date, with interest included.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Interest rates vary by bank and account. Enter the rate your own account pays; do not use a figure from a guide as your rate.

How this calculator works

The calculator first grows your current savings with compound interest, then works out the monthly deposit that covers the remaining gap over your chosen number of months.

Worked example

Target 10,000, current savings 1,000, rate 4% a year, 3 years: your current savings grow to about 1,127, and you need to deposit about 248 per month.
